# Typink privacy policy

*Last updated: 30 September 2026*

Typink doesn't collect, keep or share any information about you.

- **Your notes stay yours.** Notes, drawings and photos live in your notes folder on your Mac
  (`~/Documents/Typink` unless you chose another) and, while you work, on your iPad.
- **No servers.** The iPad app talks only to the Typink app on your own Mac, directly over your
  local network. Nothing passes through a server, ours or anyone else's.
- **No accounts, analytics, ads or tracking.**
- **Permissions, and why:**
  - *Local Network* (Mac and iPad): to find your Mac and connect to it.
  - *Camera* (iPad, only when you use these features): to scan the pairing code on your Mac and
    to take photos for your notes. Photos you pick or take are sent to your Mac to be added to
    your note, and nowhere else.
- **Updates.** The Mac app checks GitHub (github.com/remilemire/typink) for new versions. GitHub
  sees that request like any download, under [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).
- **TestFlight.** If you test the iPad app through TestFlight, Apple may share crash reports and
  any feedback you choose to send, under Apple's TestFlight terms.

Questions: open an issue at <https://github.com/remilemire/typink/issues>.
